About RenSher

What is RenSher Enterprises?

RenSher Enterprises LLC is a Florida company that builds custom websites, e-commerce and portfolio sites, and related hosting/care for Florida and U.S. businesses. We work from clear packages (Launch, Grow, Commerce) plus optional Care — and straight communication, not template lock-in.

What are the differences between Launch, Grow, and Commerce?

Launch — up to 10 pages; first credible local site with foundational SEO and Maps. Grow (recommended) — up to 20 pages with advanced SEO, blog, chat/calendar, forms — set up to help turn visitors into inquiries. Commerce — up to 35 pages with payments, APIs, in-depth SEO, and site support in three languages. Optional Care adds monthly support with a clear minutes allowance. See Offers for the full matrix and prices.

What payment options do you offer?

Package pricing includes an initial build fee plus either optional Care (monthly) or without Care (one-time maintenance-free total). Final payment schedule is set in your Service Provision Agreement.

What is Care?

Care is an optional monthly module (not a fourth package size) for ongoing hosting and support. Include Care on a package card to add the monthly price and a labeled allowance of minutes for minor updates — Launch 15, Grow 25, Commerce 45 minutes / month. Without Care, you choose the one-time without-Care total.

How should I think about price vs. other shops?

We publish our package prices in USD so you can start a conversation with clear numbers. Final scope is confirmed in the contract. We do not guarantee SEO rankings or ROI, and we do not use aggressive “% savings” marketing claims.

How we build

How does the design process work?

We scope the job, gather your materials, design and build iteratively, then launch with hosting/Care options as agreed. Timelines typically run ~30 / ~40 / ~60 business days for Launch / Grow / Commerce, subject to the Agreement.

What is custom web development?

We build to your business — structure, content, and features scoped in the proposal — rather than forcing a one-size theme. You keep clarity on what is included before signing.

What is responsive design?

Your site is built to work across phones, tablets, and desktops so customers can use it wherever they are.

Included in plans

Domain and hosting?

Plans include domain and hosting with a 99.9% uptime target where stated in the Agreement. Details and environments are confirmed in your proposal.

How does technical support work?

24/7 technical support for bugs and issues as defined in your package and Agreement. With Care, you also get a clear allowance of minutes for minor updates each month (15 / 25 / 45 depending on package) — not hours.

SEO — foundational, advanced, in-depth?

We set practical SEO matched to the package: foundational (Launch), advanced (Grow), or in-depth (Commerce). Rankings and ROI depend on external factors and are not guaranteed.

Blog, chat, forms, multilingual, payments?

Blog, calendar/chat, and dynamic forms start at Grow. Grow includes two languages; Commerce adds site support in three languages. Payment gateways, dashboards, and external APIs are Commerce features. See the feature matrix.

Custom & next steps
