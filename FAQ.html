FAQ

Practical answers about plans, process, and getting started.

About RenSher

What is RenSher Enterprises?

RenSher Enterprises LLC is a Florida company that builds custom websites, e-commerce and portfolio sites, and related hosting/care for Florida and U.S. businesses. We work from clear plans (Basic, Standard, Premium) and straight communication — not template lock-in.

What are the differences between Basic, Standard, and Premium?

Basic — up to 10 pages, foundation site with basic SEO and Maps. Standard (recommended) — up to 20 pages with advanced SEO, blog, chat/calendar, forms. Premium — up to 35 pages with payments, APIs, premium SEO, and deeper multilingual support. See Offers for the full matrix and prices.

What payment options do you offer?

Plan pricing includes an initial build fee plus either a maintained monthly option or a maintenance-free option. Final payment schedule is set in your Service Provision Agreement.

How should I think about price vs. other shops?

We publish our plan prices in USD so you can start a conversation with clear numbers. Final scope is confirmed in the contract. We do not guarantee SEO rankings or ROI, and we do not use aggressive “% savings” marketing claims.

How we build

How does the design process work?

We scope the job, gather your materials, design and build iteratively, then launch with hosting/care options as agreed. Timelines typically run 30 / 40 / 60 business days for Basic / Standard / Premium, subject to the Agreement.

What is custom web development?

We build to your business — structure, content, and features scoped in the proposal — rather than forcing a one-size theme. You keep clarity on what is included before signing.

What is responsive design?

Your site is built to work across phones, tablets, and desktops so customers can use it wherever they are.

Included in plans

Domain and hosting?

Plans include domain and hosting with a 99.9% uptime target where stated in the Agreement. Details and environments are confirmed in your proposal.

How does technical support work?

24/7 technical support for bugs and minor changes as defined in your plan and Agreement (change allowances differ by tier).

SEO — basic, advanced, premium?

We set practical SEO foundations matched to the plan. Rankings and ROI depend on external factors and are not guaranteed.

Blog, chat, forms, multilingual, payments?

Blog, calendar/chat, and dynamic forms start at Standard. Multilingual expands from basic (2 languages) to professional translation (3) on Premium. Payment gateways, dashboards, and external APIs are Premium features. See the feature matrix.

Custom & next steps

When should I choose a custom build?

When your needs do not map cleanly to Basic/Standard/Premium. Describe only what you need and we will quote that scope. Use Customize your build or the customizer.
